About FLUENT

A single, considered hand
through an unfamiliar country.

Moving to Tokyo is not a transaction. It is a life — one that touches family, work, education, and the quiet daily rituals that make a place feel like home.

FLUENT exists in the space between finding a residence and building a life. For international families, founders, and investors, we hold every thread — property, company, schooling, wealth, culture — so that arrival feels less like relocation and more like belonging.

Based in Minato and Shibuya, we know these neighbourhoods the way a resident does: which street is quiet at dusk, which school suits which child, where a family finds its footing. That knowledge is the service.
